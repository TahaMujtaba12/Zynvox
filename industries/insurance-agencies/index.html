Questions

Insurance agencies: FAQ.

What does Zynvox do for insurance agencies?

When a call to your insurance agency is missed, Zynvox replies in seconds by text (or answers by AI voice), qualifies the caller, books the appointment, logs everything to your CRM and sends reminders — 24/7.

Does Zynvox quote prices?

No. It gathers the details and books time with a licensed agent, who provides the quote.

Can it remind clients about renewals?

Yes. Renewal reminders go out on the schedule you set.

How quickly can we go live?

It starts with a 20-minute analysis call, where we map your call flow and calculate your missed-call revenue leakage. We then configure Zynvox on your existing business number.
