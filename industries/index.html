Questions

Industries FAQ.

Does Zynvox work for my industry?

If customers call your business to book, buy or ask for help, yes. The workflow — answer, qualify, book, log, follow up — is the same; your rules decide the questions, priorities and bookings.

What changes from one industry to another?

The qualification questions, what counts as urgent, what gets booked (a visit, a consultation, an appointment, a demo) and which follow-ups are sent. The platform and its ten modules stay the same.

My industry isn’t listed. Can Zynvox still help?

Very likely. Book an analysis call and we will map your call flow and calculate your missed-call revenue leakage from your own numbers.
