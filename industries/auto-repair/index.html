Questions

Auto repair shops: FAQ.

What does Zynvox do for auto repair shops?

When a call to your auto repair shop is missed, Zynvox replies in seconds by text (or answers by AI voice), qualifies the caller, books the appointment, logs everything to your CRM and sends reminders — 24/7.

Can it book around our bay capacity?

Yes. Bookings follow the capacity and hours you set, so the shop isn’t overbooked.

Does it send service reminders?

Yes. Oil-change and service-interval reminders go out automatically based on the last visit.

How quickly can we go live?

It starts with a 20-minute analysis call, where we map your call flow and calculate your missed-call revenue leakage. We then configure Zynvox on your existing business number.
