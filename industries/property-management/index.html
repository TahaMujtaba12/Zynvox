Missed-call automation for property management companies.

Zynvox answers every missed call to your property management company within seconds — by text or AI voice — qualifies the caller, books the appointment, updates your CRM and sends reminders, 24 hours a day. Unanswered maintenance calls turn small problems into expensive ones, and leasing enquiries go to other buildings.

Questions

Property management companies: FAQ.

What does Zynvox do for property management companies?

When a call to your property management company is missed, Zynvox replies in seconds by text (or answers by AI voice), qualifies the caller, books the appointment, logs everything to your CRM and sends reminders — 24/7.

Can Zynvox handle emergency maintenance at night?

Yes. Your rules define what counts as an emergency; those requests reach your on-call vendor immediately.

Does it work for leasing enquiries too?

Yes. Prospects are qualified on move-in date, budget and unit type and booked for viewings.

How quickly can we go live?

It starts with a 20-minute analysis call, where we map your call flow and calculate your missed-call revenue leakage. We then configure Zynvox on your existing business number.
