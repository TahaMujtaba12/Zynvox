Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Zynvox  ·  taha@zynvox.tech

01

Who We Are

Zynvox ("we," "us," or "our") builds Operational Intelligence Infrastructure for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical service businesses. Our platform is accessible at zynvox.tech.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways — information you provide directly, and information collected automatically.

Information you provide:

  • Name and business name when booking a consultation call
  • Email address when submitting a contact form or inquiry
  • Phone number if provided during outreach or onboarding
  • Business operational data (call volume, ticket size) shared during analysis calls

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on page, and referral source
  • IP address and general geographic location
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and conduct analysis calls and product demonstrations
  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested information
  • Improve our website, platform, and service offerings
  • Send relevant follow-up communications related to your inquiry
  • Analyze website performance and user behavior

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

04

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We use reputable third-party services for scheduling (Cal.com), form handling (Formspree), and analytics — each operating under their own privacy policies.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05

Third-Party Services

Our website and operations use the following third-party services:

  • Cal.com — consultation booking and scheduling
  • Formspree — contact form submissions
  • Chatbase — AI-powered business inquiry system
  • LinkedIn — company presence and outreach

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies independently.
