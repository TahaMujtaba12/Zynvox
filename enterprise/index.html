In short

Call automation at network scale.

Zynvox Enterprise runs the same missed-call workflow across every location — answer, qualify, book, log, follow up — while each location keeps its own rules and leadership sees recovered calls, bookings and revenue across the whole network.

Built for scale

What changes when you have many locations.

Rules per locationEach location keeps its own hours, service area, pricing rules, urgency rules and team — on one platform.
Routing across the networkCalls and leads are routed to the right location, team or on-call person based on address, service and availability.
One view for leadershipThe Owner Dashboard shows missed calls recovered, response times, bookings and estimated revenue across locations.
Your systems stay the source of truthEvery lead, conversation and booking is logged to your CRM or field-service system automatically — no re-typing.
Shared business memoryThe knowledge graph learns from outcomes across the network, so what works in one location improves qualification and routing in others.
Consistent customer experienceEvery caller gets the same fast, on-brand reply, whichever location they call and whatever time it is.

Questions

Enterprise FAQ.

Does Zynvox work for franchises and multi-location businesses?

Yes. Each location has its own rules, hours and team, while leadership sees results across the network in one dashboard.

Can Zynvox integrate with our CRM or field-service software?

Zynvox logs every lead, conversation and booking to your system of record. We confirm the exact integration for your stack during the analysis.

How do we measure the impact?

Start with a pilot group of locations. The dashboard tracks missed calls recovered, response time, bookings and estimated revenue, so you can compare them with locations not yet live.

What about security and data handling?

Customer data is processed only on your instructions and never sold. See our security page, and send us your security questionnaire during the evaluation.
