About Zynvox

Every missed call becomes a booked job.

We build AI receptionist and workflow automation systems for businesses that run on calls.

What we do

Zynvox answers the calls your team can’t. It picks up live with a natural AI voice, or replies by text within seconds when a call is missed. It qualifies the customer in a real conversation, books the appointment into your calendar, updates your CRM, notifies the right person and sends reminders — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Behind every conversation, Zynvox builds a memory of your business: your customers, your pricing and service rules, your team and what actually converts. Every interaction makes the next decision better.

Who it’s for

Any business where customers call to book, buy or ask for help — from owner-operated companies to multi-location teams. For example:

  • Plumbing companies
  • Electrical contractors
  • Roofing contractors
  • Law firms
  • Dental practices

  • Auto repair shops
  • Property management companies
  • Insurance agencies
  • Med spas & salons
  • Moving companies
  • B2B sales teams

Why it matters

Customers who can’t reach a business rarely wait — they call the next one. In a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 US consumers, 82% said they will call a competitor if a business doesn’t answer. Every unanswered call — after hours, during a job, in a meeting or on a busy morning — is revenue walking out the door.

Zynvox closes that gap without adding staff — so you keep the job, the customer and the review.
