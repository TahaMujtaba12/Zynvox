Missed-call revenue leakage.

What it is, how to calculate it, and how to stop it — in any industry.

By Taha Mujtaba, Founder of Zynvox

The short answer

Missed-call revenue leakage is the revenue a business loses when a customer calls, can’t get through, and buys from a competitor instead. Estimate it as missed calls per month × share that would have booked × share who call a competitor × average job value × 12. Not every missed call is a lost sale, so the share that would have booked matters as much as the call count. It is stopped by responding within seconds, every time.

Why it happens in every industry

Customers call when they need something now: a burst pipe, a new legal matter, a toothache, a house they want to see. If nobody answers, most don’t leave a voicemail — they call the next business on the list.

Calls are missed for ordinary reasons: the team is on a job or with a client, it is after hours, lines are busy, or the phone is on silent. None of that is a lack of effort; manual answering simply can’t cover every hour of every day.

The formula

Annual leakage = missed calls / month × share that would have booked × share who call a competitor × average job value × 12

Missed calls per month come from your phone system’s call log. The share that would have booked removes spam, wrong numbers, existing customers and price shoppers — use your normal booking rate on answered calls. Average job value is your average invoice or deal size. The share who call a competitor is the proportion of callers who give up and go elsewhere. In a 2025 CallRail consumer survey, 82% of consumers said they will call a competitor if a business doesn’t answer.

A worked example

Take an example business with 31 missed calls a month, of which 30% were real jobs that would have booked, 82% of those callers going to a competitor (the CallRail figure) and a $350 average job: the loss is about $2,669 a month, or $32,029 a year.

Your numbers will differ. Use the calculator below with your own call volume and ticket size.

How to stop it

Speed decides who wins the customer, so the fix is a workflow that responds instantly and finishes the job without staff:

  • Answer live with an AI voice receptionist when your team can’t pick up.
  • Send a text-back within seconds for any call that is still missed.
  • Qualify the caller in a real conversation: what they need, how urgent, where.
  • Book the appointment directly on your calendar.
  • Log the lead, conversation and booking to your CRM automatically.
  • Notify the right team member and the owner.
  • Send reminders and follow-ups so the customer shows up and comes back.
